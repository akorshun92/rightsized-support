Frequently asked questions

What is free and what is paid?

The game is free to download. Free: the Orientation (Synergy's guided first two quarters of Studio Acquisition), Demo Day (Ship It, Golden Parachute, Survive the Board), Daily Rightsizing and the Market Trends leaderboards. One purchase, Studio Acquisition: Enterprise License, unlocks the full Studio Acquisition campaign in all six genres. No subscription, no ads.

I bought the license on another device. How do I get it here?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, then open Settings or Procurement in the game and tap "Restore purchases." The license unlocks again at no extra cost.

My Orientation stopped after the second quarter.

That is the end of the free trial. Buying the license continues the same run from where it paused.

Family Sharing and Ask to Buy

If a family organizer has to approve the purchase, the game shows it as awaiting approval and unlocks once it is approved.

The leaderboards are missing.

Leaderboards need Game Center. Sign in under Settings → Game Center on your device and reopen the game.

Where are my saves?

On your device only. Deleting the game deletes your saves.

Is my workplace in the game?

All companies, products and people in Rightsized are fictional. Any resemblance to your actual workplace is deeply unfortunate.
