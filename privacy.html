Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Rightsized: Tech CEO Simulator ("Rightsized", "the game") is a single-player game for iPhone and iPad, developed by Oleksandr Korshun. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

Summary

We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send anything to servers that we run.

Data Stored On Your Device

Your current run, your progress (stars, achievements, tutorial progress) and your settings, such as sound and music, are stored only on your device, inside the game's own storage. They are not transmitted to us or to any third party, and they are removed when you delete the game. We cannot see them.

In-App Purchase

The Studio Acquisition: Enterprise License is sold through Apple's App Store, and Apple processes the payment through StoreKit. The game only receives a confirmation from Apple that the license is owned on your Apple Account, so it can unlock the content and restore it on your other devices. We never receive or store your payment information.

Game Center (optional)

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game submits your Demo Day and Daily Rightsizing scores to its leaderboards and shows other players' scores. Game Center is operated by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. You can sign out of Game Center in iOS Settings at any time; the game works the same without it, minus the leaderboards.

Children's Privacy

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us using the address below.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address and the "Last updated" date above will be revised.
